FlyMarket – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026

This policy explains what information the game FlyMarket ("the App"), published by Nguyễn Văn Linh, collects and why. The App is intended for users aged 13 and over.

Information we collect ourselves

None. The App does not ask for your name, email, or any account. Your best score and your sound settings are stored only on your device and are deleted when you uninstall the App.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, AdMob may collect and process information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, approximate location derived from your IP address, and ad interaction data.

  • In the EEA, UK and other regions where it is required, the App asks for your consent before any ad is requested, using Google's consent form. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy options.
  • If you do not consent to personalised ads, Google may still show non-personalised ads, which use limited data.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android device settings.

See how Google uses data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Tips (Google Play in-app purchases)

You can optionally leave a tip from the menu. Payments are processed entirely by Google Play: we never see your card or payment details. Google shares with us only an order record (product, date, order ID) needed to complete the purchase. Tips do not change the game. Google's privacy policy applies: policies.google.com/privacy.

Leaderboard (Google Play Games Services)

If you sign in to Google Play Games, your Play Games profile name and your scores are sent to Google to show the leaderboard. This is optional; the game works fully without signing in. Google's privacy policy applies: policies.google.com/privacy.

Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

We may update this policy; the date above will change when we do.
